# Riding creatures

The player who owns a creature can ride and control it once it is an adult. This works for every
species, including species added by datapacks, and does not require a saddle.

The owner is the player who released the creature from a capsule or was assigned when its egg
hatched. Other players cannot mount or control it.

## Mounting

Use an empty hand or hold a non-food item, then **right-click** the adult creature. A creature cannot
be mounted while another player is already riding it.

Food interactions take priority, so right-clicking with food feeds or breeds the creature instead.
Sneak-right-click also bypasses mounting.

## Controls

| Input | Action |
| --- | --- |
| <kbd>W</kbd> / <kbd>S</kbd> | Move forward / backward |
| <kbd>A</kbd> / <kbd>D</kbd> | Turn and move left / right |
| Mouse | Set the creature's facing direction |
| <kbd>Space</kbd> | Gain extra height while riding a flying creature |
| <kbd>Shift</kbd> | Dismount |

Flying and aquatic creatures follow the rider's view angle: look upward to climb and downward to
descend. Land creatures remain on the ground and use their species movement speed.

## Restrictions

* Only the creature's owner can mount it.
* Baby creatures cannot be ridden.
* Only one rider is supported at a time.
* Riding does not change the creature's owner or require an equipment item.

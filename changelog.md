## 26.1.2.5

- Updated `Pteranodon` texture 
- Added `Ankylosaurus` with six animations, six sounds, breeding, grazing, herding, territorial defense, and a tail-club attack.
- Added `ancientcreature:armored_herbivore` behavior profile for slow animals that stand their ground instead of charging.
- Added `Deinonychus` with seven animations, six sounds, and hunger-aware group hunting.
- Added `ancientcreature:pack_predator` behavior profile for social predators that herd, share retaliation targets, call before chasing, and leave hunting to adults.
- Added `Brachiosaurus` with seven animations, six original sounds, herding, canopy browsing, and a defensive tail sweep.
- Added `ancientcreature:browse_leaves` behavior component and `ancientcreature:sauropod_browser` profile for tall herbivores that feed from tree canopies without breaking leaf blocks.
